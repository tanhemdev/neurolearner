Ready to play?

Steer the orange buddy and pop the bubble you're asked to find. Falling off is fine: you just pop back up.

Camera video is processed on this device by MediaPipe and is never recorded or uploaded.

Let's find your comfy range

Hold your hand up so the camera can see it, then move it slowly left and right, only as far as feels comfortable.

Paused

The summary stays on this device. Copy it if a therapist or teacher wants to track progress.

Hand controls

  • Steer: move your hand left or right inside your calibrated range.
  • Jump: pinch thumb and finger (or open your hand, or raise it, in Settings).
  • Pause: press Esc or P.

Keys, touch and switches

  • ← → or A D to move, Space or Enter to jump.
  • Single switch? Turn on Auto-walk in Settings and the switch only has to jump.

Why it exists

Most learning games assume a child can hold a controller. NeuroLearner started at TherapyTales, a speech and swallow therapy clinic in Mumbai, to make a game where a webcam and whatever movement a child has is enough.
